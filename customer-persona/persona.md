🧍‍♀️ User Persona: The Cultural Eater Abroad

⸻

👤 Basic Profile

Attribute	Description
Name	Amina (example persona name)
Age	25–40 years old
Occupation	Working professional, student, or parent
Location	Recently moved to a new country (e.g., Egyptian in Germany, Indian in the UK, Nigerian in Canada)
Tech Familiarity	Moderate to high — uses smartphones and apps daily
Lifestyle	Busy, health-conscious, values convenience but emotional connection to food
Languages	Comfortable in English (and native language), prefers culturally adaptive UI/language tone


⸻

💡 Background & Context

Amina recently moved abroad for work or studies.
She’s trying to adapt to life in a new country — new food systems, new grocery stores, new routines — but food from home remains deeply tied to her identity and emotional wellbeing.

She wants to:
•	Eat healthier (e.g., lose weight, eat more balanced meals)
•	Maintain connection to her cultural cuisine
•	Simplify her daily routine (no time to plan every meal)
•	Adapt recipes to available ingredients in her new environment

⸻

🎯 Goals

Goal Type	Description
Primary Goal	Eat culturally familiar food while following a healthy diet
Secondary Goals	Simplify weekly meal planning, reduce stress about what to cook
Emotional Goal	Feel connected to her roots while building a new life abroad
Practical Goal	Automatically get weekly meal plans and grocery lists suited to her diet and location


⸻

⚙️ Behaviors & Habits
•	Shops at local grocery stores but often struggles to find traditional ingredients
•	Searches YouTube or Google for recipes but finds them too generic or non-adaptive
•	Has tried calorie-tracking apps or diet apps, but found them too Westernized or not culturally relevant
•	Cooks at home most days but lacks time to plan meals
•	Often improvises or skips healthy meals due to planning fatigue

⸻

🧠 Pain Points

Category	Problem
Cultural disconnect	Most diet apps ignore her cultural cuisine, recommending foreign meals she doesn’t relate to
Planning overload	Meal planning every week is mentally draining
Ingredient mismatch	Recipes online call for ingredients unavailable or expensive in her new country
Time pressure	Working full-time or studying leaves little time for planning balanced meals
Emotional fatigue	Misses food from home and feels a loss of cultural identity


⸻

💖 Motivations

Motivation	Description
Cultural connection	Wants to keep her traditions alive through food
Health & wellness	Wants to feel better and maintain energy, possibly follow medical/dietary needs
Convenience	Prefers automation — doesn’t want to think about what to cook
Discovery	Likes discovering lighter or modern twists on her traditional dishes
Empowerment	Feels confident cooking in a new environment when guided properly


⸻

🌈 What Success Looks Like for Amina
•	She opens the app once per week, clicks “Generate Plan,” and gets a 7-day culturally relevant meal plan.
•	The recipes feel familiar, but adapted to local ingredients.
•	She gets an email every morning: “Today’s dish — light lentil stew inspired by home 🇪🇬.”
•	She can click “Get shopping list”, go to the store once, and be done.
•	After a few weeks, she starts feeling healthier and emotionally fulfilled because she’s cooking food that reminds her of home.

⸻

📱 How She Interacts with the App

Moment	Interaction	Expectation
Onboarding	Selects diet + culture + goals	“This app understands my food heritage”
Weekly Plan Generation	Clicks one button to create plan	“Give me a full week of familiar, healthy meals”
Viewing Recipes	Reads instructions adapted to local ingredients	“Oh, I can find that at my local store!”
Shopping List	Exports ingredients list	“I want to buy everything in one trip”
Daily Reminder	Gets an email or push message	“Nice — I don’t have to decide what to cook tonight”


⸻

🧩 Psychological Insight (Why She’ll Stay)
•	Trust: The app “gets” her — not just her macros, but her culture.
•	Comfort: It recreates a piece of home while she adapts to a new world.
•	Ease: Everything is automated (plan, list, reminders).
•	Pride: Cooking food from her culture feels like self-expression, not restriction.


🧱 Summary Statement

Amina is a culturally rooted, health-conscious professional adapting to a new country.
She wants to maintain a connection to her culinary traditions while eating healthier and saving time.
She values automation, familiarity, and emotional connection — not calorie counting.
The Diet Planner empowers her to eat well, feel at home, and live balanced in her new environment.
