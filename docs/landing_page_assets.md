# Landing Page Visual Asset Brief

The new hero section uses temporary placeholders for imagery while we await final artwork. Designers can use the guidance below to deliver production-ready assets. Place the exported files in `frontend/public/assets/` with the listed filenames (or update the component paths accordingly).

## Required Assets

1. **Hero Family Dinner**  
   - **Filename:** `hero-family-dinner.jpg`  
   - **Purpose:** Primary hero background.  
   - **Concept:** Warm, candid photo of a multicultural family sharing a meal at home. Emphasise intergenerational connection (e.g., young professional with parent/elder) and a table featuring a mix of traditional dishes beside modern touches.  
   - **Mood & Palette:** Soft natural lighting, warm spice tones (terracotta, saffron, cumin). Avoid harsh contrast; aim for cosy, welcoming vibes that harmonise with the new primary `#b44c3b` and secondary `#f0a202` colours.  
   - **Framing:** Landscape orientation, ample negative space on left side (for future overlays), depth-of-field with focus on faces and shared dishes.

2. **Hero Market Basket**  
   - **Filename:** `hero-market-basket.jpg`  
   - **Purpose:** Secondary inset image layered over the hero (bottom-right).  
   - **Concept:** Close-up of a market tote with culturally diverse ingredients (spices, fresh herbs, seasonal produce). Include subtle hints of localisation (e.g., European product labels) to reinforce “global roots, local pantry.”  
   - **Mood & Palette:** Complementary to the hero photo—earthy textures, warm accents, soft sunlight.

3. *(Optional)* **Workflow Accent Illustration**  
   - **Filename:** `workflow-stepper.png`  
   - **Purpose:** Decorative option for the “How it works” section if we want to replace the current chip icons.  
   - **Concept:** Minimal line illustration showing steps of onboarding → AI planning → cooking. Use the brand palette with simple, inclusive character silhouettes.

## Technical Specifications

- Format: Optimised JPEG or WebP at 1600px width (hero) and 900px width (inset). Keep file sizes <250 KB (hero) and <120 KB (inset) if possible.  
- Cropping: Provide both full-resolution source and optimised exports so we can adjust in CSS if needed.  
- Accessibility: Supply short alt-text suggestions that reflect the scene (e.g., “Family of three sharing Moroccan dishes at a Berlin apartment table”).

Feel free to share work-in-progress for feedback—we can iterate on lighting or composition to ensure the visuals feel culturally rich and welcoming.
